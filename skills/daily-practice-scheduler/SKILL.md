# Daily Practice Scheduler Skill

## Overview
Maintains consistency and long-term retention by tracking daily problem logs and scheduling targeted reviews.

## Operations
1. Validates daily challenge structure (`day-XXX/problem.md`, `solution.cpp`, `notes.md`).
2. Calculates cumulative solved days, active streaks, and topic distribution.
3. Identifies past unsolved or difficult problems for spaced-repetition refresh.
4. Generates motivational milestones and structured study roadmaps.
